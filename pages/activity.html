Audit Log Retention & Purge (administrator only)

Audit trails grow forever if never pruned and quietly eat the free 500 MB database. Purge old entries here — by days, weeks, months or years — after exporting them. Safe workflow: ① Export (portable JSON — restorable any time) → ② Purge older than the chosen period. The purge itself is written into the log, and the log keeps every entry until you do this.
