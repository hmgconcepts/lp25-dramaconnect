How Messaging Works

  • WhatsApp (individual): opens WhatsApp with the member's number and your message pre‑filled — just press send.
  • WhatsApp (group): opens each member in turn (or copies the text) so you can paste it into your department WhatsApp group.
  • Email (group): opens your email app with everyone added as BCC (privacy‑safe) and the subject/body pre‑filled.
  • 100% free — uses your own WhatsApp/email. No paid API, no message limits from us.
