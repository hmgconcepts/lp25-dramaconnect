Getting started

Two short checklists. Tick them once and the whole department runs smoothly.

If you are a member

  1. Sign in with the email and password you were given.
  2. Open My Profile — upload a photo and complete your details (aim for the meter to reach 100%).
  3. Change your password the first time you sign in.
  4. Open My ID Card and print or save it as PDF.
  5. Check My Dashboard each time you sign in — it lists your rehearsals, tasks and messages.
  6. Install DramaConnect on your phone so it opens in one tap.

If you are an administrator

  1. Approve everyone waiting on Roles & Status → Pending Review — nobody sees anything until you do.
  2. Set branding on Settings → Global App Branding.
  3. Open Admin Data → Drive, paste your Google OAuth Client ID and take the first backup.
  4. Open Platform Health and confirm at least two heartbeat sources are fresh — one is a single point of failure.
  5. Set retention on the Storage Manager so old logs are purged safely.
  6. Create your first Production, then cast it and schedule rehearsals.
