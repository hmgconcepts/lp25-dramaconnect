How your card is checked

  • QR code (back) — any phone camera opens a secure verification page showing your photo and whether the card is valid right now.
  • Barcode (front) — Code 128 of your member number. Rehearsal and programme check-in desks scan it with a phone camera or a USB/Bluetooth scanner.
  • If the card is lost, an administrator can re-issue it: the old QR stops working immediately.
