🎨 Global App Branding

The name, organisation, logo and theme colour every member sees in the sidebar, the ID card and the public pages.

Branding is stored in the database (tenant_settings), so every device sees it. Leave the logo empty to keep the built-in RCCG logo.

🏢 Organisation & device profile

Department details for this deployment. The device profile is stored on this device only (it labels printed documents and the dashboard greeting).

The language and time zone are organisation-wide defaults; each member can still override the language from the sidebar.

♿ Appearance & accessibility

Per-device display preferences. Nothing here changes what other people see. High contrast can also be switched on for everyone by default below.

Keyboard users: every control here is reachable with Tab, and the strong focus outline makes the current control obvious. Screen readers announce section headings and status pills.

📍 Attendance & venue

The call time and grace period used by Analytics to tell "on time" from "late", and the optional venue fence that proves a member was physically present when they checked themselves in.

Idle sign-out & emergency lockdown

Idle auto sign-out and the emergency lockdown live on Platform Health → Security state because they change what happens to other people, and that page shows the live enforcement state.

🧩 Module access (hide sections from members)

Untick a section to hide it from members and unit leaders. Administrators always keep every section, and the core administration pages can never be hidden — a mistake here cannot lock you out of Settings.

Members who open a hidden section are returned to their dashboard with an explanation. Hidden sections are marked "off" in the admin sidebar.

🤖 Assistant & help

The built-in helper explains every page and answers questions about the data on screen. It is rule-based — it runs entirely in the browser with no AI service, no API key and no data leaving the device.

Open Help & FAQ
